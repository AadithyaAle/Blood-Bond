BLOOD & BOND

Instructions & Core Mechanics
  1. Wave 1 starts with minimum 3 zombies. Zombie counts and difficulty increase per wave. Boss arrives at wave 4.
  2. Father's max health increases by 20% per wave (multiplicative). A permanent boss buff increases father's max health at boss spawn.
  3. If Father's health ≤ 10%, the Son takes the weapon automatically and fights alone (auto-fire). Father regenerates until he reaches 50%.
  4. Father heals passively while recovering and receives extra HP when the Son kills infected.
  5. Buffs spawn periodically (damage, invisibility, health). Picking a buff requires sacrificing oxygen (father) or food (child).
  6. Gun damage and child protection scale with waves. During a boss fight, for every 10% Father HP lost the Child gains +5% protection and Gun damage gains +10% per 10% lost.
  7. When Father reclaims the weapon, the Son returns it. If both die, the game restarts automatically.
